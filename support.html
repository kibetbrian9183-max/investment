Frequently Asked Questions

How do I recharge my account?

Open Recharge from your profile and follow the payment instructions.

How do I activate an investment?

Make sure you have enough available balance, then select an investment plan and tap Invest Now.

How do I withdraw?

Open Withdraw from your profile, enter your account details and the amount you want to withdraw.

My transaction has a problem. What should I do?

Contact PrimeVest Support and provide your transaction details so the issue can be reviewed.
